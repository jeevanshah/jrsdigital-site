Our Flagship Comparison Engine

Why use the JRS Digital Deals Engine?

Most comparison sites promote 6-month intro rates that double later and hide network restrictions. We built an engine designed around mathematical honesty and full technical disclosure.

True 1st-Year Outlay

We compute promo months plus ongoing months across 12 full months. 28-day mobile plans are normalized to 13 renewals per year so you see what you actually spend.

CGNAT & Network Filtering

Essential for home servers, gaming, and remote work. Filter telcos with free CGNAT opt-out, static public IPv4, 30-day cancellation lock-in warnings, and 40+ Mbps upload.

Cheaper Hidden Routes

We track direct telco portals and verified partner campaigns. If a provider offers a cheaper rate on a partner link than on their own site, we show you the lower price.

Zero Paid Rankings

No telco can buy a higher ranking on our tables. Results are ordered strictly by transparent math (lowest first-year cost). All pricing is verified daily from official CIS docs.
