Bundle Intelligence

NBN & Mobile SIM Bundles: Are they worth it?

Telcos like Aussie Broadband, Superloop, TPG, and SpinTel offer $5 to $15/month discounts when pairing home broadband with mobile SIMs. However, bundling is only cheaper if the mobile plan matches your real data needs. In many cases, pairing a standalone budget SIM (such as Boost, ALDImobile, or Felix) with a standalone low-cost NBN promo results in a lower overall household bill.
