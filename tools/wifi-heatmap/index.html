Why Australian Homes Have Wi-Fi Dead Zones

Double Brick Attenuation

Standard Australian double brick and concrete block walls absorb RF frequencies heavily, reducing 5GHz speeds by up to 75% across just two walls.

Doorways & Hallways

Door openings create line-of-sight RF channels down central hallways. Placing your router with direct hallway visibility maximizes signal across bedrooms.

Mesh Node Halfway Rule

Never place a mesh booster in the dead zone itself. Place it halfway between your main router and the weak room so it can receive and re-broadcast a strong signal.
