NBN 100 Speed & Plan FAQ

Can ordering an NBN 100 plan trigger a free FTTP fibre upgrade?

Yes. Under NBN Co's residential fibre upgrade initiative, premises currently served by copper FTTN or FTTC can receive a free upgrade to full fibre (FTTP) when ordering an NBN 100 plan or higher through participating providers such as Aussie Broadband, Superloop, Leaptel, and Tangerine.

What is the difference between NBN 100/20 and NBN 100/40?

Both plans offer up to 100 Mbps download speed. NBN 100/20 provides up to 20 Mbps upload, which is plenty for video calling, cloud backups, and standard home use. NBN 100/40 provides up to 40 Mbps upload, ideal for content creators, Twitch streamers, and uploading large files for remote work.

What is a competitive price for NBN 100 in 2026?

Introductory promotional rates for NBN 100/20 generally sit between $75 and $85 per month for the first 6 months. Standard ongoing prices range from $89 to $105 per month.

Is NBN 100 worth the extra cost over NBN 50?

For households with 3 or more heavy internet users, large game downloads (e.g. 50GB+ updates), or regular remote work video conferencing, the extra $10 to $15 per month for double the download speed offers a noticeable improvement in network responsiveness.
