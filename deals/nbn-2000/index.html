NBN 2000 Speed & Plan FAQ

What is NBN 2000?

NBN 2000 is NBN Co's multi-gigabit tier, available since the September 2025 speed upgrade. It offers download speeds up to about 2000 Mbps, with 100 to 500 Mbps upload depending on the plan.

Who can get NBN 2000?

Only homes on Fibre to the Premises (FTTP). Your NBN connection box may need replacing with a newer model that supports multi-gigabit speeds; your provider arranges this when you order.

What equipment do I need for NBN 2000?

A router with a 2.5 Gbps or faster WAN port and Wi-Fi 7 or wired multi-gig Ethernet. Most laptops and PCs have 1 Gbps ports, so a single wired device will top out around 940 Mbps.

Is NBN 2000 worth it?

For most homes, no. NBN 500 or NBN 1000 covers heavy streaming and gaming for a fraction of the cost. NBN 2000 suits households with many heavy users, large daily uploads or downloads, or a multi-gig home network.

How do you rank these plans?

By first-year cost: the promo price for the promo months, then the ongoing price for the rest of the year. Referral fees never change the order. Prices come from each provider's own plan pages and are checked daily.
