NBN 50 Speed & Plan FAQ

Is NBN 50 fast enough for 4K streaming and working from home?

Yes. A single 4K stream on Netflix or YouTube requires about 15–25 Mbps. An NBN 50 connection provides 50 Mbps download, easily supporting two simultaneous 4K streams while someone else works on Zoom or browses the web.

What is a fair price to pay for NBN 50 in Australia in 2026?

Introductory 6-month promo prices for NBN 50 generally range between $65 and $75 per month. Ongoing standard rates usually sit between $79 and $89 per month. Switching providers every 6 months saves between $120 and $240 each year.

Why do NBN 50 plans increase in price after 6 months?

Most Australian retail service providers offer promotional discounts for the first 6 months to attract new customers. Once the promo expires, the plan automatically reverts to the standard ongoing rate unless you switch to another promo deal.

Which NBN 50 providers have no lock-in contracts and no 30-day cancellation notice?

Providers like Aussie Broadband, Leaptel, and Tangerine do not require lock-in contracts or 30-day cancellation notices, offering pro-rata billing when you switch. Other providers like Superloop, TPG, iiNet, and Dodo require a 30-day written notice.
