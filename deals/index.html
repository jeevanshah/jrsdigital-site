Frequently Asked Questions & Consumer Guide

Essential advice for comparing NBN and mobile plans in Australia without getting caught by marketing traps.

Which Australian NBN providers offer free CGNAT opt-out?

Aussie Broadband, Leaptel, Superloop, Tangerine, More Telecom, and Neptune Internet provide free dynamic public IPv4 addresses or one-click CGNAT opt-outs in their portals or customer support. Telstra and Optus assign public dynamic IPv4 addresses by default.

Other providers like Arctel, Flip, Dodo, SpinTel, and Vodafone charge $5/month for a static public IP, while TPG and iiNet charge $10/month.

What is the 30-day cancellation notice trap?

Several Australian telcos (notably Superloop, Exetel, Swoop, Dodo, TPG, iiNet, and SpinTel) enforce a strict 30-day written cancellation notice policy in their Critical Information Summaries (CIS).

When you switch (churn) to another provider, your old provider continues charging you for 30 full days after your cancellation request. By contrast, providers like Aussie Broadband, Leaptel, and Tangerine have no notice period and provide pro-rata refunds.

How do 28-day prepaid mobile plans quietly cost you more?

A 28-day prepaid recharge cycle renews every 4 weeks. Because there are 365 days in a year, a 28-day plan renews 13 times per year, not 12.

A plan advertised at $30 for 28 days actually costs $390/year (equivalent to $32.50/month), charging you an entire extra recharge compared to a standard calendar monthly plan. Our tracker automatically normalizes these to exact monthly-equivalent prices.

What is the difference between the full Telstra network and Telstra Wholesale?

Telstra operates two distinct mobile footprints:

  • Telstra Full Retail Network (99.6% population coverage): Used directly by Telstra and exclusively by Boost Mobile. Provides the largest regional, rural, and remote coverage footprint in Australia.
  • Telstra Wholesale Network (98.8% population coverage): Used by MVNOs like ALDImobile. Excellent in metro and regional towns, with a slightly smaller reach in deep bushland.
Are 365-day annual SIM cards worth it?

Yes. 365-day long expiry packs (such as ALDImobile $95/year for 30GB, or amaysim, Boost, Kogan annual packs) allow you to pay once upfront and receive a full year of data without monthly recharges. For light-to-moderate data users, this often reduces mobile costs to under $8–$15 per month.

How does the on-device "What do you pay now?" calculator protect privacy?

The price comparison input is stored strictly in your browser's local storage (localStorage). It is never sent to our servers, never logged in analytics, and never included in URL share links. Your personal spending data remains 100% on your device.

How does anonymous community plan verification protect my privacy?

When you submit a 10-second plan verification or speed audit, we only record the answers you choose (speed rating, billing accuracy, state, and optional feedback). We do not require accounts, names, or email addresses. IP addresses are processed transiently in-memory solely to compute a temporary daily rate-limiting hash preventing automated bot spam, and are never stored or written to disk. Raw feedback notes are retained for up to 180 days (6 months) for moderation before being purged into aggregate-only community scores.
