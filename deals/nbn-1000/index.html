Gigabit NBN 1000 Speed & Plan FAQ

Can every Australian address get Gigabit NBN 1000?

No. NBN 1000 (Ultrafast) is only available on Fibre to the Premises (FTTP) and select Hybrid Fibre Coaxial (HFC) connections. Copper connections like FTTN, FTTC, and Fixed Wireless cannot support gigabit speeds.

What Wi-Fi hardware do I need to take full advantage of NBN 1000?

To achieve 800–1000 Mbps, you need a modern Wi-Fi 6 or Wi-Fi 6E router, CAT6 or CAT6A ethernet cables, and gigabit-capable network adapters on your devices. Older Wi-Fi 5 routers typically cap real-world wireless throughput around 300–450 Mbps.

What is a competitive price for NBN 1000 in Australia?

Promotional introductory pricing for NBN 1000 currently starts around $99 to $115 per month for the first 6 months. Standard ongoing rates range from $119 to $149 per month.

How much can I save by switching NBN 1000 plans every 6 months?

Because NBN 1000 promos often carry a $20 to $30 per month introductory discount, switching between no-contract providers every 6 months saves between $240 and $360 each year compared to remaining on the loyalty rate.
